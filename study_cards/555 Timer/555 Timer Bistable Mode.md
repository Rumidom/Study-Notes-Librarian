# 555 Timer Bistable Mode
![Bistable Mode Circuit](</study_cards/555 Timer/images/Bistable Mode.png>)

### Operation
In bistable mode the Discharge and Threshold pins are not used and the internal flip-flop is toggled on and off directly through the Trigger and reset pins.
