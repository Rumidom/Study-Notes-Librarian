# 74x00 - 74LS00/74HC00 
## Quad NAND gate chip

![74x00 Pinout](</study_cards/7400 Series Chips/images/74x00_package-and-pinout.png>)

### Chip technologies 

**74HC00 - HC stands for High-speed CMOS.**
- Wide operating voltage range: 2 V to 6 V
- Wide operating temperature range: –40°C to +85°C
- Propagation delay around 15 ns

**74LS00 - LS stands for Low-Power Schottky.**
- Inputs Can Accept 3.3-V or 2.5-V Logic Inputs
- Full Military Temperature Range of –55ºC to 125ºC
- Propagation delay around 10 ns
- Inputs Are TTL Compliant; VIH = 2 V and VIL = 0.8 V
