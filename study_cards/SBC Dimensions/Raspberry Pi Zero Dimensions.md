# Raspberry Pi Zero Dimensions

![Dimensions in mm](</study_cards/SBC Dimensions/images/Raspberry_Pi_Zero_Dimensions.png>)

### Raspberry Pi Zero form-factor
The raspberry Pi Zero form factor is 65 mm wide and 30 mm tall, with 23 mm by 58 mm  screw holes, it is popular amongst single board computer manufacturers.
